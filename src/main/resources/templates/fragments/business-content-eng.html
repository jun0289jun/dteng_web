ESC(Electrostatic Chuck) Repair

Heater bonding | Plate De-bonding

DT ENG INC. performs a systematic operational process based on a total of 20 detailed steps outlined in the standard operating procedure. This is the schematized repair standard process derived from the operational manual.

Process No. Plate Type Coating Type
Step 0 Product Reception : Confirmation of incoming products
Step 1 Analysis and Evaluation of Damage / Plate Type Determination : Analysis and evaluation of product damage, planning process based on Plate Type
Step 2 Plate De-bonding : Release of bonding joints Coating Stripping : Progression of existing coating stripping
Step 3 Plate Bonding : Bonding of plate joints ESC (Emergency Stop Circuit) Coating : Application of new ESC coating
Step 4 Processing : Establishing optimal process conditions through processing
Step 5 Pattern Formation : Formation of functional patterns of the electrostatic chuck
Step 6 Product Cleaning : Removal of impurities or foreign substances
Step 7 Verification and Evaluation of Product : Verification of quality and checking compliance with standards
Step 8 Product Packaging : Packaging of finished products
